# EpisodeModBot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 24, 2026**

## 1. About EpisodeModBot

EpisodeModBot is a privately operated, non-commercial application developed by Tarly Bots using Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit).

Its purpose is to support community engagement and moderation by forwarding new submissions from r/Episode to designated Discord channels.

## 2. Information Collected

EpisodeModBot processes the following information associated with Reddit submissions:

- Reddit usernames
- Post IDs
- Post titles and text content
- Post URLs
- Submission timestamps
- Post flairs
- Associated media URLs and thumbnails
- Spoiler and NSFW indicators
- Post deletion, modification and removal status

EpisodeModBot does not request Reddit passwords, private messages or payment information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed exclusively to:

- Notify designated Discord communities of new Reddit submissions.
- Display submission information in formatted Discord messages.
- Prevent duplicate notifications.
- Synchronize changes to original Reddit submissions.
- Maintain the functionality and security of the application.

EpisodeModBot does not sell personal information or use it for advertising, profiling or AI training.

## 4. Information Sharing

Submission information is transmitted to designated Discord channels through Discord webhooks, subject to Reddit's authorization.

These channels may be accessible to ordinary members of the receiving Discord communities, not exclusively moderators.

Reddit and Discord operate under their respective privacy policies:

- [Reddit Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy)
- [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy)

Processing by these platforms may involve international data transfers.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

EpisodeModBot retains only information necessary for its authorized functionality, including identifiers linking Reddit submissions to corresponding Discord messages.

Forwarded notifications may remain visible while the original Reddit submission remains available and retention continues to serve the application's approved purpose.

Information is removed when it is no longer necessary, when required by Reddit, upon valid deletion requests or when the application permanently ceases operation, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Content Modification and Deletion

EpisodeModBot is designed to synchronize modifications, deletions, removals and relevant visibility changes affecting original Reddit submissions.

Corresponding Discord messages and associated stored information will be updated or removed as promptly as possible in accordance with Reddit's Developer Terms.

Users may also contact the developer directly to request modification or deletion of their information.

The developer cannot guarantee removal of independent copies or screenshots created by third parties outside the application's control.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect processed information.

Access credentials and Discord webhook URLs are kept confidential and are not publicly distributed.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), individuals may have the right to:

- Access their personal information.
- Request correction or deletion.
- Request restriction of processing.
- Object to processing.
- Request data portability where applicable.
- Lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below.

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality, applicable laws or Reddit's developer requirements.

The latest version will remain publicly accessible in this repository.
